Affiliate Guide
Step-by-step guide to earn commissions from Amazon, Myntra & Flipkart.

Amazon Associates India

1

Sign Up

Go to affiliate-program.amazon.in and click "Sign Up". Use your existing Amazon account.

2

Enter Website Details

Enter your website URL (your MCW site). Describe it as a men's fashion blog & product review site.

3

Get Approved

Amazon approves within 1-3 days. You need to make 3 sales within 180 days to stay approved.

4

Get Affiliate Links

Search any product on Amazon → click "Get Link" in the top bar → copy the link → paste it in your affiliate product here in admin.

5

Commission Rates

Fashion & Clothing: 9% | Shoes: 9% | Watches: 9%. Payments via bank transfer every month.

Myntra Affiliate Program

1

Sign Up via Admitad or vCommission

Myntra runs through affiliate networks. Sign up at vcommission.com or admitad.com and search for Myntra.

2

Apply to Myntra Program

Inside the network, apply to the "Myntra" affiliate program. Approval takes 2-5 days.

3

Generate Links

Once approved, go to any Myntra product page, use the network's link builder tool to create your affiliate link.

4

Commission

Men's fashion: up to 10%. Payments monthly via bank transfer when you cross ₹1000 threshold.

Flipkart Affiliate Program

1

Sign Up

Go to affiliate.flipkart.com and register with your email and website details.

2

Get Links

Log in → go to any product → click "Affiliate Links" → copy and paste the link into your affiliate product below.

3

Commission

Clothing & accessories: up to 10%. Footwear: up to 12%. Minimum payout ₹250.

📸 How to get Product Image URL from Amazon

1

Go to Amazon product page

Open any product on Amazon India that you want to add to your website.

2

Right-click on the product image

Right-click on the main product photo → click "Copy image address" or "Copy image link".

3

Paste in Image URL box above

Paste the copied link in the "Product Image URL" field above. It usually starts with https://m.media-amazon.com/images/...

4

Get your affiliate link

Go to Amazon Associates → SiteStripe bar at the top of Amazon → click "Text" or "Image+Text" → copy the link with your tag mcwfashion-21
